How we work

Six steps, from the first session to well after launch, each with something concrete you receive.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We sit with you and your team and understand the problem before we talk about tools.

    What you getA written summary of the challenge, in your own words.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    We map the current process in numbers and find where time is lost and errors pile up.

    What you getA map of your current process, showing where time and money leak.

  3. 03

    Design

    We design the simplest solution that treats the cause, not the symptom, and agree a clear scope with you.

    What you getA solution concept and a scope agreed in writing.

  4. 04

    Build

    We build in short steps you can see and try, so there are no surprises at the end.

    What you getA working version you can try yourself, not a slide deck.

  5. 05

    Launch

    We launch with training and knowledge transfer, so the solution becomes yours.

    What you getA documented launch, training for your team, and full knowledge transfer.

  6. 06

    Improve

    We track the impact after launch and improve where the numbers point.

    What you getA post-launch impact report and a prioritised list of improvements.

About Mahlool

Who we are

Mahlool is a Saudi business and technology solutions company. We start with consulting to understand your business, then analyse processes, develop solutions, automate procedures, connect systems and turn data into KPIs and reports. We also build our own products, including Marfof for warehouse management.

Our vision

To be the first choice for technology solutions in the region, and to help build a more efficient, smarter future for business.

Our mission

To enable companies to reach their goals through innovative, easy-to-use technology solutions focused on creating real, sustainable value.

The name

Mahlool (محلول) comes from the Arabic root h‑l‑l: to untie, to resolve. Our name is our promise: a clear resolution to every business challenge.
